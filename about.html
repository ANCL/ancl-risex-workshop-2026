What are ROS 2 and PX4?

ROS 2

The robotics framework connecting your controller to the drone’s state and carrying its commands.

PX4

The autopilot estimating the drone’s state and handling low-level flight control.

What you’ll take away

  • Understand how ROS 2 and PX4 work together for Offboard control.
  • Build a basic controller using vehicle state and a trajectory reference.
  • Connect a simulated test to a real flight experiment.
